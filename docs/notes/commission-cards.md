# Commission cards catalogue

Researched 2026-10-17. Ticket: PER-140. This is private research. Client names and card text in this
file must never appear in the app. The app only learns the patterns at the bottom.

## Source and coverage

- All 77 cards in the table come from one page: https://dressmakerwiki.site/commissions (build 410 data).
- The tag counts on https://dressmakerwiki.site/style-tags match a count made from the table, tag by tag.
  The two pages agree.
- The rule types (minimum, ceiling, avoid, only, share, count, trim length, different colours) are
  explained at https://dressmakerwiki.site/style-tags.
- cozygameguides.com did not turn up in search, so it was not read. Search found other guide sites
  (keengamer.com, games.gg, allthings.how) that describe the same card shape. One of them gives Edith's
  first card as Daywear, Glamour, Risqué and at least one brooch, which matches row 5 below.
- Numeric tag values, Quality and budget are left out on purpose.
- The wiki shows no difficulty for a card. The table keeps the wiki's own order, which I take to be
  story order (the wiki's /walkthrough page was not read to confirm). Difficulty can be read from how
  many tags and extra rules a card has.

## How to read the table

- Tags: the style tags the card asks for. "(max)" means the card puts a ceiling on that tag, so it is
  closer to "not too much of it" than to a request. The wiki lists these inside the tag list.
- Extras: colours, fabrics, accessories and counts. "Only" means every fabric must be one of the listed
  colours, or the listed fabric. "N%" is a share of the dress. A bare number on a sequin row is as the
  wiki prints it and the unit is unclear.
- Row 64 is a branching card. The player's earlier answer decides which tags apply.

- Six tags are very common, at 16 to 18 cards each: Gothic, Glamour, Romantic, Cute, Playful, Elaborate.
- Elegant and Risqué follow at 13. Five more tags sit at 9 or 10.
- Casual and Shimmering never appear. Uncomfortable appears once (row 19).
- Tags per card: 2 tags on 22 cards, 3 on 36, 4 on 12, 5 on 1. Three is the usual.
- Ceilings ("not too much of this tag") appear on 4 cards (8, 17, 22, 69). They act like a style avoid.

- Three clusters: sweet (Cute, Playful, Romantic, Patterned), dark and showy (Gothic, Glamour, Risqué,

- The hand-written opposing pairs (spec section 7) are rare on real cards. Cute and Gothic share three

  Formal, Warm with Cool and Daywear with Eveningwear never share a card.

### Colours

- "Only these colours" on 10 cards (3, 27, 30, 31, 41, 50, 52, 69, 72, 77). Lists run one to three
  colours.
- "Avoid a colour" on 2 cards: Black (row 2) and White (row 17).
- A share of one colour on 9 cards, from 25% to 75%.
- "At least N different colours" on 4 cards (6, 40, 49, 63), with N of 3. One branch of row 64 says 5.
- Colours asked for, by card count: Black 8, White 4, Green 4, Yellow 3, then Red, Pink, Orange, Grey
  and Blue at 2, then Brown and Purple at 1.

### Fabrics

- "Only this fabric" on 4 cards: Silk twice (39, 74), Cotton (73), Velvet (41).
- A share of a fabric on 12 cards, from 10% to 60%.
- Fabrics asked for, by card count: Velvet 4, Sequin 4, Silk 3, Lace 2, Wool 2, Cotton 2, Linen 1.
  Velvet and Sequin cards are mostly Gothic, Glamour and Risqué cards.
- No card avoids a fabric type.

### Accessories

- 19 of 77 cards (about a quarter) set an accessory count. Every one is "at least". None is "exactly".
- No card avoids an accessory.
- By type: Jewel 6 cards, Bow 5, Feather 3, Tassel 2, Brooch 2, Flower 2, Bell 1.
- Counts seen:

| Type | Counts |
|---|---|
| Jewel | 1, 3, 5, 5, 10, 10 |
| Bow | 2, 3, 3, 5, 5 |
| Feather | 4, 5, 10 |
| Tassel | 5, 5 |
| Brooch | 1, 1 |
| Flower | 3, 5 |
| Bell | 3 |

- Counts of 1 to 5 are the norm. 10 is the ceiling and appears on 3 cards (51, 59, 76).
- Trim lengths on 5 cards (7, 29, 56, 61, 75): 10cm, 20cm and once 100cm. Lace Trim is the most common
  trim ask. The app drops trim lengths, as the spec says.
- Appliqué, Button and Piping are never asked for by name. They still add to tags.

### What this tells us

- Weight tags by the card counts, in bands (see `data-v1-leanings.md`).
- Most real cards mix three tags. A challenge with three directions reads like a real commission.
- Avoids are rare and are mostly colours. Style avoids exist as ceilings. Fabric and accessory avoids
  never appear, so `fabricAvoid` and `accessoryAvoid` are our own addition. Keep them to Medium and above.
- "Only" rules are on 13 cards, about one in six, mostly colours. That supports `colourOnly` on Hard
  and Chaos.
- Counts are all "at least", from 1 to 10, mostly 2 to 5. The spec's exact counts (Chaos only) are our
  own twist, not something the game asks.
